    How students use it

    1. Open this page and choose My Turn. Add #student to the link to show only that tab.
    2. Type the class code from the board and their name exactly as on your roster.
    3. Set their life (housing, car and so on) from their tracker, then tap Roll.
    4. Record the card in the tracker. Forks: pick A or B and write one sentence why.
    5. Rerolls cost 2 Skill Credits and must show in the ledger.

    Week 1 is a practice roll. Weeks 2–8 are Build Rounds (one Build card each). Round Days run Weeks 9–18, and Weeks 16–18 draw from every week's cards.
