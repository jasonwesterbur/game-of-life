How it works

  1. Each round, every student gets 1 Life Event, 3 upgrade cards (they keep 1) and up to 3 dice rolls for the Life Choice Menu.
  2. You can draw for anyone on the Draw tab. Click the upgrade a student keeps so it shows in the Class Log.
  3. Students can draw themselves: share this page's link with #student on the end. They type the class code, their name and the round.
  4. The draws always match. They're calculated from the class code, the name and the round, so refreshing never gives a new card.
  5. In the News is one card per round for the whole class.
  6. Record on the tracker as usual: Life Event in "Life Event", a Use Now card in "Use now Card", and Keep or Protection cards in their rows.

The Life Event deck can be Mixed (all themes) or one theme, like Credit or Smart Buyer, to match the week's lesson. Students pick the same deck you announce.
