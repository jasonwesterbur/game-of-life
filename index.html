How a turn works

  1. Each student sees one Keep, one Use Now and one Protection card. They keep one of the three.
  2. Then they read their Life Event and record it in the Life Event rows.
  3. Keep and Protection cards last the whole game. Use Now cards count only this round.
  4. Protection and Keep cards show the chance a matching Life Event comes up, so students can make a smart choice.
  5. Dice are for the Life Choice Menu only, with up to 3 rolls a round.
  6. Students draw on their own: share this page's link with #student on the end.

Draws come from the class code, the student's name and the round, so every screen shows the same cards. Click + Add a period to set up another class, and switch with the Period menu at the top.
